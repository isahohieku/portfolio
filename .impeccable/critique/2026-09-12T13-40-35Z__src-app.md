## Overall Impression

Strong typographic instincts and one genuinely well-designed component, wrapped around a portfolio that shows none of its work, cannot be operated by keyboard or screen reader, is information-free on touch, and has no contact mechanism. The biggest opportunity is not a redesign but pointing the existing design at evidence already in the codebase.

### [P1] Mobile nav is not an accessible dialog, and the scroll lock is written but inert
header.component.html declares role="dialog" aria-modal="true", then: focus never moves to the panel; tabbing reaches page content behind the overlay on the 3rd Tab; Escape is bound to the overlay div so once focus leaves the hamburger the menu cannot be dismissed by keyboard; the scrim carries a stray tabindex="0". The hamburger button wraps a single empty span -> NO accessible name, announced as "button", measuring 32x24px.
Scroll lock root cause (found by neither sub-agent): header.component.ts:22 adds body.menu-open and header.component.scss:186 defines body.menu-open{overflow:hidden}, but the component uses default ViewEncapsulation.Emulated, so the served bundle contains `body.menu-open[_ngcontent-%COMP%]` and <body> never carries a component content attribute. The rule is authored, compiled, and can never match -- which is why scrollY reached 900 with the menu open.
Fix: aria-label + aria-expanded, pad to 44x44; move focus into panel on open and restore on close; trap Tab in .sidemenu; keep the document-level @HostListener Escape (header.component.ts:33) and drop the template binding; move body.menu-open to styles.scss or use :host-context; remove tabindex="0" from the scrim.
Command: /impeccable audit
